# Econia v5.9.0 — Visual intelligence cockpit

## New experience

- Country flags are resolved dynamically and displayed before country names.
- Regions, partners and economic groups receive distinct visual identities instead of fake flags.
- Internal geography codes are translated into readable French labels.
- Extraction results and the persistent observations page now offer two complementary views:
  - an exhaustive analytical table;
  - a responsive gallery of economic-event cards.
- Cards expose geography, status, indicator, value, unit, period, fact type and source at a glance.
- Hover elevation, status rails and responsive layouts make dense results easier to scan.
- File upload, alerts, section dividers and table hover states have been completely restyled.
- Existing navy, coral, teal and ivory brand colours are preserved.

## Safety boundary

No ingestion, extraction, semantic-binding, temporal-resolution, Arabic-processing or validation service was modified.
